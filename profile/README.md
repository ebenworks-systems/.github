# Ebenworks

Ebenworks is an AI product company building for the people the system overlooks. Nine products on one shared platform.

[ebenworks.co](https://ebenworks.co) · [About](https://ebenworks.co/about) · [Work](https://ebenworks.co/work) · [Research](https://ebenworks.co/research) · [Open source](https://ebenworks.co/open-source) · [Careers](https://ebenworks.co/careers) · [Contact](https://ebenworks.co/contact)

## Products

### [Imali AI](https://imali-ai.ebenworks.co/)

Imali AI is a business operating system for African shops and small teams: sales, invoices, stock, staff and books from a WhatsApp chat, with web and Android.

[How it works](https://imali-ai.ebenworks.co/how-it-works/) · [Capabilities](https://imali-ai.ebenworks.co/capabilities/) · [Pricing](https://imali-ai.ebenworks.co/pricing/) · [FAQ](https://imali-ai.ebenworks.co/faq/)

### [Chingu Care AI](https://chingu-ai.ebenworks.co/)

A Korean voice companion that talks with a senior living alone at one press and tells their family when something changes. The family never sees the words.

[Product page](https://ebenworks.co/products/chingu)

### [Phila Health Ecosystem](https://phila-health.ebenworks.co/)

Seven products for practices, clinics, outreach, programmes, districts, hospitals and learning. Offline care records in the shared app. A clinician decides.

[Practice management](https://phila-health.ebenworks.co/practice-management) · [Clinic management](https://phila-health.ebenworks.co/clinic-management) · [Hospital management](https://phila-health.ebenworks.co/hospital-management) · [Clinical basis](https://phila-health.ebenworks.co/clinical-basis)

### [BidWright Tender AI](https://bidwright-ai.ebenworks.co/)

Turn tender documents and company evidence into a requirements report and an editable response. Review missing evidence and claims before submission.

[Demo](https://bidwright-ai.ebenworks.co/demo) · [Tender compliance checklist](https://bidwright-ai.ebenworks.co/guides/tender-compliance-checklist) · [Company evidence library](https://bidwright-ai.ebenworks.co/guides/company-evidence-library) · [For IT services](https://bidwright-ai.ebenworks.co/for/it-services)

### [Diaspry Community OS](https://diaspry.ebenworks.co/)

Everything a diaspora association needs to run itself: a branded site, member registry, events, news, a photo archive and committee roles.

### [Izwi Music OS](https://izwi-os.ebenworks.co/)

The software that runs an independent record label: public site, fan store, catalogue, contracts, royalty splits, payouts and artist logins.

### [Tzohar Sites](https://tzohar-sites.ebenworks.co/)

Personal websites for creatives and founders. We design, write, build, launch and maintain the site, in up to five languages.

### [A–Z Dates](https://az.dates.ebenworks.co/)

Twenty-six date ideas per city, one for every letter of the alphabet. Real addresses, maps and insider tips. Free.

[South Korea](https://az.dates.ebenworks.co/korea/) · [United States](https://az.dates.ebenworks.co/usa/) · [Canada](https://az.dates.ebenworks.co/canada/) · [Seasons](https://az.dates.ebenworks.co/seasons/)
